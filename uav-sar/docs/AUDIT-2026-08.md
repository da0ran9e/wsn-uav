# Independent audit — findings and fix backlog (2026-08)

Four independent reviewers were commissioned to audit this project for
publication readiness. **One completed** (simulation correctness / residual
oracles). Three (experimental fairness, statistical rigour, publication
readiness) terminated early on an infrastructure limit and must be re-run.

This document records what the completed audit found, what it verified as
correct, and the prioritized work needed before any of these results are
published. **Claims marked ❌ below must not be used until fixed and
re-measured.**

---

## BLOCKING — invalidates a headline claim

### B1 ❌ The energy comparison measures the stop criterion, not the scheme
`uavEnergyJ` integrates rotary-wing power at 1 Hz per airborne UAV. Because
power is nearly flat over the flown speed range (hover 168.5 W, 15 m/s 123 W),
the metric collapses to `numUav × sim_duration × ~150 W`:

| scheme | numUav | sim end | energy | **E/(numUav·t)** |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| proposed | 4 | 66.2 s | 44.4 kJ | **167.6 W** |
| nocoop | 4 | 45.5 s | 27.2 kJ | **149.4 W** |
| pure-uav | 1 | 68.8 s | 10.8 kJ | **156.4 W** |
| tsp-mc | 4 | 114.9 s | 65.7 kJ | **143.0 W** |

Worse, the stop criteria are not comparable: `proposed`/`tsp-mc` stop on the BS
report, while `nocoop`/`pure-uav` stop when the **ground-truth victim node**
finishes reassembly (`sar-ground-app.cc:375`, wired at `sar-config.cc:218`).
The baselines' simulated lifetime — hence their energy and packet totals — is
set by an oracle.

Also: RESULTS-honest.md explains the gap as "two extra UAVs loiter/divert
instead of sweeping". That is **factually wrong** — `nocoop` flies the same
four UAVs.

**Fix.** Scheme-independent termination (fixed horizon, or energy-to-a-named-
identical-milestone for every arm). At minimum report energy-per-UAV-second
alongside totals so the reader sees it carries no behavioural signal.

### B2 ❌ The distributed election / SUMMON suppression cannot fire
SUMMON is single-hop and never forwarded (`sar-ground-app.cc:215-232`;
suppression at `:262-267`). Suppression between cell leaders therefore needs two
CLs inside G2G range. They never are. Default config (8×8 @ 20 m,
`kHexCellRadiusM=80`, `DeriveG2gRangeM()=37.15 m`) yields 5 cells with leaders
at pairwise distances **63.2 … 156.2 m** — every pair out of range. Structural,
not stochastic: hex centres sit `√3×80 = 138.6 m` apart while the ground radio
reaches 37 m.

Measured: **2 summons in 10/30 runs, 3 in 2/30, never suppressed.** What is
implemented is "every cell crossing ALERT summons independently". The
evidence-backoff election presented as the core control-plane contribution
never arbitrates anything.

**Fix (design choice pending).** (a) give SUMMON a TTL and flood it like SHARE;
(b) run the election on the SHARE plane, which does cross cell boundaries; or
(c) set `kHexCellRadiusM` to something the G2G radio can bridge. Then re-measure.

### B3 ❌ The BS report carries no localization information
- `REPORT` = `[type][0x00][regionId=1:u16][255]` — a constant
  (`sar-fast-uav-app.cc:184-188`, `sar-data-uav-app.cc:230-233`).
- `HANDOFF` = `[type][0xFF][regionId:u16]` (`sar-data-uav-app.cc:243-247`), so
  the FAST courier that flies the report home never receives coordinates.

The ±16 m estimate exists only in the cell leader's RAM and in `events.csv`.
The claim "a report at the BS **and** a location estimate" is two separate
things; `timeToReportAtBS_s` times a content-free token.

**Fix.** Put `[cx:i16][cy:i16]` in HANDOFF and REPORT (both far under the 100 B
ceiling), decode at the BS, add `reportedX/reportedY` to `metrics.csv`, and
measure localization end-to-end rather than at the leader.

- **m8** **Reproduction is broken**: every campaign script except
  `campaign_mc_*` targets `ns3.46-scenario-sar-default`, which does not exist

  **superseded** `summon_start` metric that this project explicitly disowned.
- **m9** Dead code that misleads: `RegionCoordinator` (192 lines) still compiled

  `channel-test.cc` prints it as if it were.
- **m10** `regionId` hardcoded to 1 everywhere ⇒ any CONFIRM stops any DATA UAV.
- **m11** ALERT is cheap in large cells: ~10 nodes at ev=0.15 union past 0.75;
  the default substrate puts 28 members in one cell.
- **m1** LoS/NLoS is nearly vestigial (foliage applied to both branches; they
  differ only by `kNlosExcessDb=10 dB [Assume]`), while the Al-Hourani constants
  describe a blockage process the model no longer represents.

## Verified correct (independent confirmation)

1. **No residual oracle in the sensing→localization chain.** No runtime
   global-view object; `RegionCoordinator` is never initialised. Victim position
   reaches the UAV only via clue field → per-node scalar → node evidence → RPT
   carrying the node's **own** GPS → leader centroid → SUMMON.
2. `m_isTarget` appears in **no decision path** — metrics and the declared
   baseline stop only.
3. Every cooperative message is a real `NetDevice::Send` on one shared LrWpan
   channel; the helper-lifetime destructor trap is avoided.
4. All packets fit the 127 B PSDU (max 100 B app + ~13 B).
5. No flood loops, no deadlocks, all retry chains quota-bounded, long-lived
   events cancelled in `StopApplication`.
6. `PhyStats` classification matches ns-3.46 lr-wpan semantics.
7. **Seeding is correct and reproducible** (`SetSeed(1)`+`SetRun(seed)`,
   byte-identical repeats); a given seed yields the same channel across schemes,
   which is good for paired comparison.
8. Link-budget arithmetic checks out (FSPL(1 m,2.4 GHz)=40.05 dB; derived G2G
   range matches closed form; A2G ≈ −90 dBm @ 50 m).
9. **The radio is load-bearing**: spacing 15/20/30/40 m → localize fires
   100/100/90/**15** %. The connectivity-threshold result reproduces.
10. Headline delivery error reproduces: 16.4 m median / 29.1 m p90 (doc: 15.8 / 28.1).
11. Fading model correct (Nakagami m=1.5 per packet; per-pair frozen shadowing
    is the right fix for ns-3's per-packet `RandomPropagationLossModel`).

**Auditor's verdict:** *"Not yet trustworthy for publication as written — but the
foundation is sound."* The bypass-removal work is real; three headline claims
(B1, B2, B3) do not survive contact with the code.

## Statistical re-analysis (N = 100 seeds/scheme, this session)

Run with `tools/campaign_stats.py` (dependency-free; Wilson CIs for rates,
bootstrap CIs for medians, Mann-Whitney U for differences).

| scheme | mission ok | t_report | victim ok | t_victim | delivery err |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| proposed | 100 % [96.3,100] | **65.6 s** [64.6,66.7] | 97 % [91.5,99.0] | 42.6 s [41.6,43.5] | **17.0 m** [14.1,18.8] |
| tsp-mc ×4 | 100 % [96.3,100] | 114.9 s (variance ≈ 0) | 99 % [94.6,99.8] | 45.0 s [43.1,47.3] | — |
| tsp-mc ×1 | 100 % | 216.2 s | 97 % | 86.7 s | — |
| nocoop | 0 % (no report stage) | — | 99 % | 45.0 s | — |
| pure-uav | 0 % | — | 99 % | 70.0 s | — |

Mann-Whitney U, mission time, proposed vs tsp-mc ×4: z = −12.2, **p < 1e-16**.

**Two corrections this forces on the prose:**

1. **Victim service is statistically indistinguishable.** Fisher exact on
   97/100 vs 99/100 gives **p = 0.621**. Any claim of parity *or* superiority on
   victim delivery is unsupported; the t_victim CIs also overlap.
2. **The energy advantage is duration-driven, not efficiency-driven.** Average
   power is 143–170 W across all schemes; `proposed` actually draws ~19 % *more*
   per second and wins only by finishing 1.75× sooner. (Independent of, and
   consistent with, B1.)

**Incidental finding worth reporting:** `tsp-mc` completion time is essentially
deterministic (114.9017–114.9029 s across 100 seeds, ~1 ms spread) — an
open-loop schedule cannot exploit a good channel. `proposed` varies 62–69 s
because it is evidence-closed-loop. The flip side is honest too: the baseline
has a **predictable makespan bound**, a genuine operational virtue.

## Claims that still stand after audit + statistics

- ✅ Faster to mission completion: 65.6 s vs 114.9 s, p < 1e-16.
- ✅ ~5× fewer application packets (1896 vs 9576; CIs disjoint) — *subject to
  M7's counting fix and the shared-channel caveat*.
- ✅ Only scheme producing a location estimate — *subject to B3: it must
  actually reach the BS*.
- ✅ Connectivity threshold at spacing > G2G range (independently reproduced).
- ❌ Energy superiority (B1) · ❌ distributed election (B2) · ❌ end-to-end
  localization delivery (B3) · ❌ localization accuracy as a *sensing* result (M9).
